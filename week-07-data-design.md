# Summary

**In a nutshell:** Data begins with curiosity. Use it to raise better questions, stay close to people, and remember it may point at a symptom and not the cause.

## Thinking about data
- **Correlation isn't causation:** data can point somewhere that isn't the core issue. Use **5 Whys** to dig deeper.
- **Data personalities:** I love data, want it open and accessible, see design and data as belonging together, and lean towards words over numbers.
- **Data objects:** a local cheese prompted who, what, when and why questions. Anything can be read as data.
- **Data humanism** (Georgia Lupi): keep the human side of data visible.

## Context: Bergamo's circular economy
- **Goal:** break down emissions and reduce impact by 2030. The circular economy is *one* of several routes, and a low-hanging fruit compared with mobility.
- **Three pillars:** reuse the city that exists; prevent surplus and redistribute what remains; repair, reuse and consume with care.
- **In place or being tried:** reuse and recycling centre, compost bins, reused construction debris, wine corks, upcycled fabrics, coffee capsule returns, cigarette butts, e-waste collection, bags that track household waste.
- **As reported in the discussion:** about 90% of waste is segregated, and collection and processing costs €21 million.
- **Hard problems:** diapers and menstrual pads, hospital plastic, third-party companies' waste, and people's habits.
- **Hope:** the closer the municipality gets to people, the more willing they are to change.

## Method
1. Understand the domain and surface assumptions and hypotheses.
2. Do desk research and gather questions from the data.
3. Map the interview (Anja and the municipality) together as a team.
4. Narrow down, and work with a researcher.

- **Team:** Andrew, Govind and Vivek, looking at i-Foria in Pescara, which worked with elderly homes and hospitals on a collection centre.
- **Tools:** Beautiful Soup, pandas.

## Questions worth chasing
- Which materials could be eliminated entirely, and what would that change?
- How much waste is industrial, and should it stop at manufacturing?
- What is in the 28.15% organic share (FORSU)?
- What are the financial effects of reduced manufacturing and consumption?
- Do incentives improve segregation? Why not open the data?
- Could combined fabrics make reusable diapers and menstrual cups?
