## Reflection on the final exercise

- know your tools better (focusing / ISO / settings etc.)
- follow some basic shooting principles landscape vs portrait or using a tri-pod stand and take time to focus on subject
- find better angles and use light appropriately
- have someone with you to help/note-take and the other moderate the session
- 🚨 i was shooting and asking some impromptu questions on the fly (outside of the script), got hard categorise them and spoilt the narrative maybe
- 🚨 i kept the audio recording on but broke the video - damn editing is going to be pain in the arse
- my video became more about the space than the "person"

- 💡 focus on some of the personality traits of the person, their quirks, behaviours - these might help oncover nuances later in the design process
- 💡 keep in mind the background noise when shooting
